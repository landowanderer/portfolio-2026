Field Brief2026

Know what to prepare
before heading outdoors.

Field Brief is an Apple Maps redesign that turns fragmented outdoor-planning information into a proactive preparation layer.

I identified a gap between route information and the place-specific signals outdoor users need before setting out, then translated that insight into a clear priority system and an SF Symbols–based interface. I used interface motion to structure how preparation signals unfold, and AI filmmaking to communicate the feature through a cohesive product film.
